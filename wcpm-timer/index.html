WCPM Timer

WCPM Timer measures reading fluency as words correct per minute.

Privacy Policy

WCPM Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Profiles, tests, and results are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it uses no third-party services.

If you share a PDF progress report, it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting the app deletes all its data.

Last updated: October 2026.
